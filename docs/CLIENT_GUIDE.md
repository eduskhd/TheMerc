# The Merc — Website Content Guide

Welcome! This guide explains how to update your website content — events, menu items, and gallery photos — all without touching any code.

You manage everything through **Sanity Studio**, a simple content editor that works in your browser.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1 — Accept your invite

You'll receive an email from **Sanity** inviting you to the project. Click the link in that email and create a free Sanity account (or log in if you already have one).

### Step 2 — Go to your studio

Open your browser and go to:

```
https://the-merc.sanity.studio
```

> Direct link: **https://the-merc.sanity.studio/**

Log in with the account you just created. You'll see three sections on the left sidebar:

- 📅 **Events**
- 🍕 **Menu Items**
- 🖼️ **Gallery Photos**

---

## Managing Events

### Add a new event

1. Click **📅 Events** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **pencil + icon** (top right) or the **"New Event"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Artist / Event Name** — required (e.g. *Johnny Cash Tribute Band*)
   - **Event Type** — pick one: Live Music, Trivia Night, Special Event, Community
   - **Date** — required (click the calendar to pick a date)
   - **Start Time** — use 24-hour format (e.g. `20:00` = 8pm, `19:30` = 7:30pm)
   - **End Time** — optional
   - **Description** — a short sentence or two about the event
   - **Event Photo** — drag and drop a photo or click to upload
   - **Ticket URL** — paste a link if tickets are sold online (optional)
   - **Featured Event** — turn this ON to highlight the event on the homepage
4. Click **Publish** (green button, top right)

> Changes appear on the website within about 1 minute.

### Edit an existing event

1. Click **📅 Events**
2. Click the event you want to change
3. Make your edits
4. Click **Publish**

### Remove an event

1. Click **📅 Events**
2. Click the event
3. Click the three-dot menu (⋯) in the top right corner
4. Select **Delete**

## Managing the Menu

### Add a menu item

1. Click **🍕 Menu Items** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **"New Menu Item"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Name** — required (e.g. *The Merc Burger*)
   - **Menu Section** — pick one: Pizza, Burgers, Good Eats, Coffee, Drinks
   - **Description** — optional, a short description
   - **Price** — e.g. `$12` or `$10/$14` (for small/large sizes)
   - **Tags** — check any that apply: gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan, spicy, new, popular
   - **Photo** — optional, upload a photo of the item
   - **Note** — optional, for any extra info (e.g. *Ask about weekly specials*)
   - **Available on Menu** — leave this ON. Turn it OFF to temporarily hide an item without deleting it.
4. Click **Publish**

### Hide an item temporarily

If an item is out of season or temporarily unavailable:

1. Open the item
2. Turn off the **"Available on Menu"** toggle
3. Click **Publish**

The item disappears from the website but stays in your list so you can turn it back on later.

## Managing the Gallery

### Add a photo

1. Click **🖼️ Gallery Photos** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **"New Gallery Photo"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Photo** — required, drag and drop or click to upload
   - **Description** — required, a short caption (e.g. *Live music at The Merc*)
   - **Category** — pick one: Food, Drinks, Live Music, Venue, People
   - **Display Order** — a number that controls where the photo shows up. Lower numbers appear first. (e.g. put `1` for the first photo, `2` for the second, etc.)
4. Click **Publish**

### Tips for photos

- Best size: at least **1200 × 800 pixels**
- Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
- Keep file sizes under **5 MB** for fast loading

## Good to Know

**When do changes go live?**
Usually within **1 minute** after you click Publish. If you don't see the change, try refreshing the page.

**Can I break anything?**
No. You can only add, edit, or remove your own content. The website's design and layout are separate and cannot be changed from here.

**What if I make a mistake?**
Sanity keeps a full history of every change. If something goes wrong, let your developer know and it can be restored.

**Can someone else help me?**
Yes! Ask your developer to add another user to the studio. Each person gets their own login.
